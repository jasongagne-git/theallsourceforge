Opportunity of the Week

The Pentagon's creation of a Direct Reporting Program Manager for unmanned systems (DRPM-UxS) reporting directly to Deputy Secretary Feinberg consolidates authority over nearly all ground vehicles, small air vehicles, and most sea vehicles under a single autonomy czar (Breaking Defense). This reorganization creates a single point of contact for AI builders developing autonomous platforms and a centralized authority for defense professionals seeking to field multi-agent systems at scale. The timing coincides with the UK committing £5 billion to autonomous systems including a "hybrid navy" (Breaking Defense), creating parallel demand for autonomous systems integration expertise across allied nations.

The governance gap isn't slowing deployment — it's creating a documented trail of known risks that organizations are choosing to accept in favor of speed.
